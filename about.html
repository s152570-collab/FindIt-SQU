About Us

About FindIt@SQU

FindIt@SQU is a website designed to help students and staff at Sultan Qaboos University find their lost belongings.

Our website allow user to report lost or found items, browse available reports, and request to claim items that belong to them.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make lost items easier and help students and staff return belongings to their owners.

Our Team

FindIt@SQU was created as a University project to provide a simple and useful solution for lost and found items on campus.

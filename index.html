Welcome to FindIt@SQU

Lost something on campus? or found an item that belongs to someone else?

FindIt@SQU helps SQU students and staff report lost and found items, search for missing belongings, and return items to their owners.

Our goal is to make finding lost items easier, faster and safer for everyone at SQU.
